제조/생산

공장 견학, 생산 일정, 품질 문제를 다루는 Part 3, 4 대화와 Part 7 사내 공지에 자주 나온다.

manufacture [매뉴팩처]
제조하다, 제조
The parts are manufactured in Vietnam.
assembly line [어셈블리 라인]
조립 라인
The new assembly line doubled our output.
facility [퍼실리티]
시설, 공장
The company opened a new facility in Texas.
capacity [커패서티]
생산 능력, 수용 인원
The plant is operating at full capacity.
output [아웃풋]
생산량
Monthly output rose by 12 percent.
defect [디펙트]
결함
Every unit is checked for defects.
quality control [퀄리티 컨트롤]
품질 관리
She works in the quality control department.
specification [스페서피케이션]
사양, 명세
The product meets all customer specifications.
prototype [프로토타입]
시제품
Engineers will test the prototype next week.
component [컴포넌트]
부품, 구성 요소
We source components from three suppliers.
streamline [스트림라인]
간소화하다, 능률화하다
The new software streamlined the ordering process.
malfunction [맬펑션]
오작동, 고장 나다
A malfunction stopped the line for an hour.
operate [아퍼레이트]
가동하다, 운영하다
Only trained staff may operate this machine.
durable [듀어러블]
내구성이 좋은
These boots are made of durable material.
automate [오터메이트]
자동화하다
The packaging stage has been fully automated.
자주 출제되는 콜로케이션 operate at full capacity (최대로 가동하다) / meet safety standards (안전 기준을 충족하다) / streamline the process (절차를 간소화하다) / ahead of schedule (예정보다 빨리) / quality control inspection (품질 검사)

물류/배송

주문 확인 메일, 배송 지연 안내, 재고 문의는 Part 7 이메일 지문의 단골 소재다.

shipment [쉽먼트]
배송, 배송품
Your shipment will arrive on Thursday.
dispatch [디스패치]
발송하다
Orders are dispatched within 24 hours.
expedite [엑스퍼다이트]
신속히 처리하다
Can you expedite the delivery?
courier [커리어]
택배 회사, 배달원
The documents were sent by courier.
freight [프레이트]
화물, 운송료
Freight charges are included in the price.
warehouse [웨어하우스]
창고
The items are stored in our main warehouse.
supplier [서플라이어]
공급업체
We are looking for a new supplier.
carrier [캐리어]
운송 업체
The carrier lost two of our packages.
in stock [인 스탁]
재고가 있는
The blue model is currently in stock.
backorder [백오더]
이월 주문, 재고 부족으로 밀린 주문
The printer is on backorder until May.
tracking number [트래킹 넘버]
운송장 번호
Use the tracking number to check the status.
fragile [프래절]
깨지기 쉬운
Please handle fragile items with care.
surcharge [서차지]
추가 요금
A fuel surcharge applies to all orders.
estimated [에스티메이티드]
예상되는, 추정의
The estimated delivery date is June 3.
bulk [벌크]
대량
We offer discounts on bulk orders.
자주 출제되는 콜로케이션 track a shipment (배송을 추적하다) / expedited shipping (빠른 배송) / be out of stock (재고가 없다) / free of charge (무료로) / place a bulk order (대량 주문을 하다)

계약/협상

계약 갱신, 조건 변경, 합병 소식은 Part 7 기사와 편지에서 자주 다룬다. 동사와 명사 형태를 같이 익혀 둔다.

contract [칸트랙트]
계약, 계약서
Both parties signed the contract yesterday.
terms [텀즈]
(계약) 조건
Please read the terms carefully.
negotiate [니고시에이트]
협상하다
We negotiated a lower price with the vendor.
clause [클로즈]
조항
A new clause was added to the agreement.
renew [리뉴]
갱신하다
The lease must be renewed every two years.
expire [익스파이어]
만료되다
The contract expires at the end of March.
binding [바인딩]
구속력 있는
This agreement is legally binding.
terminate [터미네이트]
종료하다, 해지하다
Either party may terminate the contract.
draft [드래프트]
초안, 초안을 쓰다
I will send you the draft by Monday.
stipulate [스티퓰레이트]
명시하다, 규정하다
The contract stipulates a 30-day notice period.
obligation [아블리게이션]
의무
The supplier has no obligation to deliver early.
mutually [뮤추얼리]
서로, 상호 간에
We reached a mutually beneficial agreement.
compensation [캄펜세이션]
보상, 보수
Customers will receive compensation for the delay.
merger [머저]
합병
The merger will be completed next year.
acquisition [애퀴지션]
인수
The acquisition of the startup cost $2 million.
자주 출제되는 콜로케이션 terms and conditions (약관) / negotiate the terms (조건을 협상하다) / legally binding (법적 구속력이 있는) / the contract expires (계약이 만료되다) / mutually beneficial (서로에게 이로운)

IT/기술

시스템 점검 공지, 소프트웨어 설치 안내, 기술 지원 요청 이메일에 나오는 어휘다.

upgrade [업그레이드]
개선하다, 업그레이드
We will upgrade the network this weekend.
install [인스톨]
설치하다
A technician will install the new printer.
compatible [컴패터블]
호환되는
The app is compatible with most phones.
troubleshoot [트러블슛]
문제를 찾아 해결하다
Call IT if you cannot troubleshoot the error.
outage [아우티지]
정전, 서비스 중단

back up [백 업]
백업하다
Please back up your files before Friday.
encrypt [인크립트]
암호화하다
All customer data is encrypted.
access [액세스]
접근, 접근하다
Only staff have access to the server room.
user-friendly [유저 프렌들리]
사용하기 쉬운
The new interface is very user-friendly.
technician [테크니션]
기술자
A technician is on site to fix the problem.
functionality [펑셔낼리티]
기능
The update adds new functionality.
innovative [이노베이티브]
혁신적인
The firm is known for its innovative designs.
cutting-edge [커팅 엣지]
최첨단의
The lab uses cutting-edge equipment.
obsolete [압설리트]
구식의, 쓸모없게 된

patent [패튼트]
특허
The company holds a patent on the design.
자주 출제되는 콜로케이션 be compatible with (~와 호환되다) / technical support (기술 지원) / scheduled maintenance (정기 점검) / state-of-the-art (최신식의) / have access to (~에 접근할 수 있다)

행사/교육
