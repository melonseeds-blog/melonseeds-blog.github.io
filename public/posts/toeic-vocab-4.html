고난도 동사

Part 5 어휘 문제에서 보기 4개가 모두 동사로 나올 때 자주 보이는 단어들이다. 뜻만이 아니라 뒤에 오는 목적어, 전치사까지 같이 본다.

accommodate [어카머데이트]
수용하다, (요구에) 맞추다
The hall can accommodate 300 guests.
alleviate [얼리비에이트]
완화하다
The new road will alleviate traffic congestion.
anticipate [앤티서페이트]
예상하다
We anticipate strong demand this summer.
assess [어세스]
평가하다
The team will assess the damage tomorrow.
attribute A to B [어트리뷰트]
A를 B의 덕분(탓)으로 돌리다
She attributes her success to hard work.
consolidate [컨살러데이트]
통합하다
The two offices will be consolidated.
deem [딤]
~라고 여기다
The plan was deemed too expensive.
diminish [디미니시]
줄다, 줄이다
Interest in the product has diminished.
enforce [인포스]
(법, 규칙을) 시행하다
The new rules will be strictly enforced.
ensure [인슈어]
보장하다, 확실히 하다
Please ensure that all doors are locked.
exempt [이그젬트]
면제하다, 면제된
Small firms are exempt from the tax.
incorporate [인코퍼레이트]
포함하다, 통합하다
We incorporated your feedback into the design.
reinforce [리인포스]
강화하다
The training reinforces safety habits.
solicit [설리싯]
(의견, 도움을) 요청하다
We are soliciting feedback from customers.
waive [웨이브]
(요금, 권리를) 면제하다, 포기하다
The bank agreed to waive the fee.
adhere to [애드히어]
(규칙을) 지키다, 고수하다
All staff must adhere to the dress code.
동사 어휘 문제 푸는 법 빈칸 뒤 목적어를 먼저 본다. waive the fee, solicit feedback, accommodate guests, alleviate congestion처럼 목적어와의 궁합이 정해져 있다. 수동태 빈칸이면 be deemed, be exempt from, be consolidated가 자주 나온다.

고난도 형용사

명사 앞 빈칸이나 be동사 뒤 빈칸에 들어가는 형용사다. 비슷한 뜻끼리 묶어서 외우면 보기 사이 차이가 잘 보인다.

substantial [섭스탠셜]
상당한
The firm made a substantial investment.
comprehensive [캄프리헨시브]
포괄적인, 종합적인
We offer a comprehensive health plan.
preliminary [프릴리머네리]
예비의, 사전의
The preliminary results look positive.
feasible [피저블]
실현 가능한
Is it feasible to finish by Friday?
viable [바이어블]
실행 가능한, 존립 가능한
Solar power is a viable option.
imperative [임페러티브]
반드시 해야 하는
It is imperative that we act now.
integral [인티그럴]
없어서는 안 될
She is an integral part of the team.
prominent [프라미넌트]
저명한, 눈에 띄는
A prominent architect designed the museum.
respective [리스펙티브]
각각의
They returned to their respective offices.
stringent [스트린전트]
엄격한
The lab follows stringent safety rules.
unprecedented [언프레서덴티드]
전례 없는
Sales reached an unprecedented level.
versatile [버서틀]
다용도의, 다재다능한
This tool is extremely versatile.
conducive [컨듀시브]
(~에) 도움이 되는
A quiet office is conducive to focus.
lucrative [루크러티브]
수익성이 좋은
The contract proved highly lucrative.
accessible [액세서블]
접근 가능한, 이용하기 쉬운
The venue is accessible by subway.
형용사 짝 표현 be conducive to (~에 도움이 되다) / be integral to (~에 필수적이다) / It is imperative that S (should) + 동사원형 / preliminary findings (예비 조사 결과) / stringent requirements (엄격한 요건)

고난도 부사

900점을 가르는 문제는 부사 어휘인 경우가 많다. 문법으로는 모두 들어갈 수 있으므로, 함께 쓰이는 동사와 시제가 단서가 된다.

considerably [컨시더러블리]
상당히
Costs have risen considerably.
consistently [컨시스턴틀리]
꾸준히, 한결같이
The store has consistently ranked first.
tentatively [텐터티블리]
잠정적으로
The meeting is tentatively scheduled for May 4.
temporarily [템퍼레럴리]
일시적으로
The lobby is temporarily closed.
exclusively [익스클루시블리]
독점적으로, 오로지
The offer is available exclusively online.
approximately [어프락시미틀리]
대략
The tour lasts approximately two hours.
previously [프리비어슬리]
이전에
As previously mentioned, the office will move.
subsequently [섭시퀀틀리]
그 후에
The plan was subsequently approved.
accordingly [어코딩리]
그에 따라
Prices will be adjusted accordingly.
markedly [마키들리]
현저히
Quality has improved markedly.
readily [레딜리]
쉽게, 선뜻
Spare parts are readily available.
thoroughly [써럴리]
철저히
Please read the manual thoroughly.
periodically [피리아디클리]
주기적으로
The filters are checked periodically.
inadvertently [인어드버턴틀리]
무심코, 실수로
The file was inadvertently deleted.
solely [솔리]
오로지, 단독으로
You are solely responsible for the account.
부사와 짝이 되는 표현 increase considerably / be temporarily closed / be tentatively scheduled / be readily available / review thoroughly / adjust accordingly / be solely responsible for. 숫자 앞 빈칸이면 approximately, nearly, almost 계열을 먼저 본다.

고난도 명사

추상 명사는 앞뒤 전치사와 묶여서 관용 표현처럼 나온다. 표현 단위로 외우는 편이 빠르다.

consensus [컨센서스]
합의
The board reached a consensus quickly.
discretion [디스크레션]
재량
Bonuses are paid at the manager's discretion.
incentive [인센티브]
장려책, 성과급
The company offers incentives for referrals.
initiative [이니셔티브]
새 계획, 주도권
The recycling initiative was a success.
precaution [프리코션]
예방 조치
Take precautions when handling chemicals.
provision [프러비전]
(계약) 조항, 공급
The provisions of the contract are clear.
quota [쿼터]
할당량
Each sales rep has a monthly quota.
criteria [크라이티어리어]
기준 (criterion의 복수)
The selection criteria are listed below.
compliance [컴플라이언스]
(규정을) 따름, 지킴
The factory is in full compliance with the law.
discrepancy [디스크레펀시]
불일치
There is a discrepancy in the figures.
endorsement [인도스먼트]
지지, 보증
The product received an endorsement from experts.
inquiry [인콰이어리]
문의
We received many inquiries about the job.
reputation [레퓨테이션]
평판
The hotel has an excellent reputation.
patronage [페이트러니지]
(가게) 애용, 후원
Thank you for your continued patronage.
expertise [엑스퍼티즈]
전문 지식
We need someone with legal expertise.
명사 관용 표현 at the discretion of (~의 재량으로) / take precautions (예방 조치를 하다) / in compliance with (~에 따라, ~을 지켜) / reach a consensus (합의에 이르다) / meet the criteria (기준을 충족하다) / Thank you for your patronage (이용해 주셔서 감사합니다)
